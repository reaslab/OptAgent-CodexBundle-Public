# Test problem: regional warehouse selection

A retailer may open warehouses A, B, and C. Opening costs are 100, 95, and 80.
Their capacities are 8, 7, and 6 units. Four customers have indivisible demands
of 3, 2, 2, and 3 units. Each customer must be assigned to exactly one open
warehouse; its demand cannot be split between warehouses.

The assignment costs are:

| warehouse | customer 1 | customer 2 | customer 3 | customer 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 8 | 6 | 10 | 9 |
| B | 9 | 7 | 4 | 3 |
| C | 5 | 8 | 7 | 6 |

Choose which warehouses to open and assign all customers without exceeding
capacity. Minimize total fixed opening cost plus assignment cost.
